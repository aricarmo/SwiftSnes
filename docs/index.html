01 — PEEK

Cursor on the notch

The header shows up on its own: game title, FPS and pause state. No extra window in the Dock or the menu bar.

02 — PLAY

Click to expand

The panel slides down with the game running live. Drag a ROM in or pick one of the recents.

03 — PIN

⌘⇧P keeps the keyboard

A global shortcut captures the keyboard for the game. Esc releases it. Everything is remappable in the settings panel.

Under the hood

Full emulation, written from scratch in Swift.

65C816 CPU · PPU modes 0–7

Sprites, HDMA and every video mode, including Mode 7.

SPC700 + S-DSP

BRR, ADSR and echo. 32 kHz audio through a lock-free ring buffer, no crackle.

LoROM · HiROM · ExHiROM

Score-based header detection. Automatic SRAM saves.

DSP-1 to DSP-4

Emulated NEC µPD77C25 coprocessor. Super Mario Kart and Pilotwings run with firmware you provide.
